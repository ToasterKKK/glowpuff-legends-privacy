Glowpuff Legends: Neon RPG — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 11, 2026 (purchases added)

Glowpuff Legends ("the game") is published by Tomer Shaked ("we"). This policy explains what information the game handles.

Summary

The game has no accounts, no advertising and no third-party tracking. It does not collect your name, email, contacts or location. If you make an in-app purchase, the purchase is processed by Apple and recorded by our purchase provider, RevenueCat, under a random anonymous ID (see "Purchases" below).

Purchases

In-app purchases and the Glowpuff Premium subscription are paid through your Apple Account. We never see your payment details. To know what you bought and to restore it on another device, the game uses RevenueCat (RevenueCat, Inc.), which receives: a random anonymous app user ID, your purchase history for this game (product, date, price, country of the storefront), and basic device information such as the operating system version. This data is used only to deliver and restore purchases; it is not used for advertising or tracking. RevenueCat's privacy policy: revenuecat.com/privacy. You can manage or cancel a subscription in your Apple Account settings. To ask for deletion of your purchase record, email us.

Data stored on your device

Your game progress (character level, items, gold, pet status and settings) is saved only on your device. It never leaves your device and we cannot access it. Deleting the game deletes this data.

Children

The game does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Future changes

If a future version adds online play or advertising, this policy and the game's App Store privacy details will be updated before that version is released.
