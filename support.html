FAQ

How do I move? Drag the joystick area on the lower-left of the screen, or tap the ground to walk there.

How do I fight? Tap a monster to target it; your hero attacks automatically. Use the hotbar for spells, runes and potions.

What does Ziggy do? Ziggy the Glow-Puff follows you, bites enemies and heals you when your health is low. Keep him fed and happy.

Where is my progress saved? On your device. Deleting the game erases it.
